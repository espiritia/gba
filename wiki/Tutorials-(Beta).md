# Introduction
This is a collection of handy modifications for [pokeemerald](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald). Modifications tend to range in both size and scope and cover all different facets of the games. The goal of this collection is to share resources among all fan-game developers while also showcasing to newer developers the ease and benefits of switching to decompilation-based projects.

This is intended to be a more sustainable version of the indexes that [Avara](https://www.pokecommunity.com/showthread.php?p=9962099#post9962099) and [Anon822](https://www.pokecommunity.com/showpost.php?p=10354961) maintained. Thank you both for all of your hard work!

# [List of Tutorials / Modifications](#list)

## Before Submitting

1. PLEASE make sure your implementation works in an unchanged/vanilla version of the game.
2. PLEASE try formatting your tutorial for ease of reading. An excellent format to follow can be seen in [Rangi's Adding a Pokémon Type & Disabling the Mystery Type](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/wiki/Adding-a-Pok%C3%A9mon-Type-&-Disabling-the-Mystery-Type).
3. Choose a place to submit your tutorial. [Github is preferred](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/wiki/_new), but if you can't use GitHub, you can create [a new post in this thread](https://www.pokecommunity.com/newreply.php?do=postreply&t=416647).

## After Submitting

1. Please add your tutorial to the [table](#list). (Or ask somebody who can, like [psf](https://www.pokecommunity.com/member.php?u=930942) on PokéCommunity, or members of [#pokeemerald](https://discord.com/channels/442462691542695948/442465020291317760) in [pret](https://discord.gg/jvTP6MSK).) Adding to the index will help other developers find it quickly and easily.
2. Publicize your work! Post about it on [reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonROMhacks/submit), [Twitter](https://twitter.com/compose/tweet), or discord communities like [#pokeemerald](https://discord.com/channels/442462691542695948/442465020291317760) in [pret](https://discord.gg/jvTP6MSK) or [#decomps-disassemblies](https://discord.com/channels/157983957902819328/369552511557697537) in [PokéCommunity](https://discord.gg/KZZ6DjGbQp). Spread the good word, & thanks for the contribution! ☺️
